Final Consumer Experience

A consumer should be able to scan a product QR and immediately see:

✅ Is the product genuine?

✅ Who manufactured it?

✅ Which farms supplied it?

✅ Which lab certified it?

✅ When was it produced?

✅ Is it recalled?

✅ Is the supply chain traceable?

✅ Can I trust this product?


This is the ultimate outcome of HerbChain: turning a normal QR code into a complete Digital Product Passport, giving consumers transparent, verifiable, farm-to-product traceability in a few seconds.
